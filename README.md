# CCAT Practice

Study repository for the **CCAT** (*Criteria Cognitive Aptitude Test*), in the context of a hiring process for **Senior Staff Engineer AI-Native** (USA).

## Why this project exists

This space is for:

- gathering study materials and references about the CCAT
- practicing and reinforcing verbal, numerical, and spatial reasoning
- centralizing questions, notes, and research that come up during the process
- tracking progress and gaps before the test

This is not a product — it is a study and preparation hub for the hiring process.

## About the CCAT

The CCAT measures general cognitive aptitude, typically in ~15 minutes with ~50 questions, covering:

| Area | What it measures |
|------|------------------|
| **Verbal** | Vocabulary, analogies, word relationships |
| **Math / Numerical** | Arithmetic, basic algebra, proportions, estimation |
| **Spatial / Logic** | Patterns, sequences, mental rotation, abstract reasoning |

Scores are often used as an early filter for speed and accuracy under time pressure — not as a measure of technical seniority.

### Suggested workflow

1. **Understand the format** — timing, question types, score expectations
2. **Diagnose** — take a short mock and map weak areas
3. **Focused drills** — practice by area (verbal / math / spatial)
4. **Timed mocks** — prioritize pace and accuracy under pressure
5. **Capture learnings** — what went wrong, why, and what to review

## Process context

- **Target role:** Senior Staff Engineer AI-Native
- **Location:** USA (abroad)
- **Role of this repo:** cognitive prep + organization of study/research for the process — not a substitute for technical preparation (system design, AI systems, leadership, etc.)

## Study principles

- Prefer **timed practice** over passive reading
- Log mistakes with a cause (concept vs. rush vs. misread)
- Review recurring patterns, not only isolated questions
- Keep notes short and actionable in `notes/` and `research/`
