# Fraction Division

**Area:** numerical — theory  
**Added:** 2026-08-09

## What it means

Dividing fractions answers: *“How many times does this smaller fraction fit into the other?”*

---

## The main trick: flip and multiply

You do **not** divide fractions directly. Instead:

1. **Flip** the second fraction (swap numerator and denominator).
2. **Multiply** the two fractions.

**Example:** `3/4 ÷ 1/2`

1. Flip `1/2` → `2/1` (which is 2)
2. Multiply: `3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = 3/2`

**One-line rule:** *Divide fractions = flip the bottom one and multiply.*

**Mnemonic (Keep, Change, Flip):**

- **Keep** — leave the first fraction as is
- **Change** — replace ÷ with ×
- **Flip** — invert the second fraction

---

## Why it works (intuition)

Think of a **chocolate bar** split into equal pieces.

- `3/4` = you have **3 pieces out of 4**
- `1/2` = half the bar = **2 pieces out of 4**

Question: *How many halves fit in `3/4`?*  
Answer: **1 whole half plus one more piece** → `1 1/2` halves.

Flip-and-multiply gives the same result without drawing every time.

---

## Step-by-step examples

### Example 1: `2/3 ÷ 1/4`

1. Keep `2/3`
2. Flip `1/4` → `4/1`
3. Multiply: `2/3 × 4/1 = 8/3` (or `2 2/3`)

### Example 2: `5/6 ÷ 2/3`

1. Keep `5/6`
2. Flip `2/3` → `3/2`
3. Multiply: `5/6 × 3/2 = 15/12`
4. Simplify (divide top and bottom by 3): `5/4`

---

## Extra tricks

### 1. Simplify before multiplying

If a numerator and a denominator share a common factor, **cancel first**:

`5/6 × 3/2`

- 6 and 3: divide both by 3 → `5/2 × 1/2 = 5/4`

Fewer steps, fewer mistakes.

### 2. Whole number ÷ fraction

Write the whole number over 1:

`3 ÷ 1/2 = 3/1 ÷ 1/2`

Flip `1/2` → `2/1`, multiply: `3/1 × 2/1 = 6`

**Mental shortcut:** dividing by `1/2` = **multiplying by 2** (how many halves in 3? Six.)

### 3. Fraction ÷ whole number

`3/4 ÷ 2 = 3/4 × 1/2 = 3/8`

**Mental shortcut:** the denominator **doubles** (4 → 8); the numerator stays the same.

---

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| Divide numerator by numerator, denominator by denominator | Flip the **second** fraction and multiply |
| Skip simplifying at the end | Always check for cancellation |
| `1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/8` | Correct: `1/2 × 4/1 = 2` |

---

## Pocket summary

```
a/b ÷ c/d  =  a/b × d/c
```

1. First fraction — **leave it**
2. Second fraction — **flip it**
3. **Multiply**
4. **Simplify** if possible
